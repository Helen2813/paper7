Duration prediction & DMSO gate

200 GENES · RIDGE 0.25 · VARIANCE FLOOR 0.25
Two-stage evaluation

Stage 1: pooled training DMSO to detect activity. Stage 2: time-matched training DMSO + ridge amplitude. Both are evaluated on heldout batches; drug identity is supplied.

Requires raw counts or logCPM, control times, and ≥2 train replicate pairs per fold.
Interpretation boundaries

Target = known elapsed time since an experimentally recorded intervention. Not hidden transcriptional onset, and not a clinical test. A one-batch training fold is an adaptation of V115, not unchanged independent validation.
